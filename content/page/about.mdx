## who i am

i'm adam. technical training got me in the door: a two-year diploma in cyber defence and cloud administration, a 3.8 GPA, and a 5th place finish at a provincial skills competition. everything past that, i learned on my own.

this site is where all of that lives: what i break, what i learn, what i build, and what i want to pass on to whoever's standing where i used to be.

## what i solve

**break it first**: bug bounty, vulnerability research, pentesting. finding the blind spot nobody else caught is the whole point.

**build it back**: soc work, detection engineering, incident response. catch it, then make sure it can't happen the same way twice.

**brainstorm it new**: tooling, automation, grc. the stuff that makes everything else faster, cleaner, and easier.

## how i work

i got into this because i was curious, stubborn, and hungry for knowledge. never thought i'd be debugging and applying fixes at 2am, driven by whatever mix of desire and spite it took to find the answer.

that drive means i don't quit easily. if i don't get something, i ask, i research, i test, i hammer away until a solution appears. when i get it, i run with it, fold it into what i already know, and apply it to the next problem.

i can't stand just existing. i want to be useful, use my time well, communicate clearly, be impressive, and chase the goal over being right.

i don't see other people as competition. the only opponent i have is myself from the day before. everyone else is dealing with the same challenge i am. stumbles, conflict, miscommunication, stress are part of the process, not something i take personally.
